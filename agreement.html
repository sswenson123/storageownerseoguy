Services Agreement

The Storage Owner's SEO Guy · Version 2026-11-20.1 · Written in plain English on purpose. Both sides should actually be able to read their own agreement.

This Agreement is between Scott Swenson, doing business as The Storage Owner's SEO Guy (the "Provider"), and the business owner who accepts it during signup (the "Client"). The Client's name, business, and address are the ones entered during signup.

1. Services and Fees

  • What the Client is buying: the services selected during signup and shown on the order summary. Service descriptions are in Schedule A.
  • Standard prices: Website Build $199 per facility (one-time); Maintain plan $59 per month per facility; Maintain + Improve plan $99 per month per facility.
  • Promo codes: any promo code discount is the one validated and shown on the order review the Client approves before payment, or confirmed in writing when monthly billing begins.
  • Invitation prices: if the Client signed up through a private invitation issued by the Provider, the invitation prices shown on the order summary apply instead; the amount charged by the Provider's payment processor and the Provider's own invitation records control if they ever differ from what a page displayed.
  • When monthly billing starts: monthly plans begin on the date the new website goes live (or on the date of signing, if no Website Build is purchased). Payment details are collected at signing or at the start of monthly service; the first monthly payment is charged on the day the website goes live, and renews on that same day each month. Monthly fees are billed in advance.
  • No contract, no cancellation fee: there is no long-term contract and no cancellation fee. The Client may cancel any monthly plan at any time, effective at the end of the current billing month.
  • Switching plans: the Client may switch between the Maintain plan and the Maintain + Improve plan at any time, in either direction, as often as the Client likes, by asking the Provider (email counts). There is no fee to switch. The new plan and its monthly price (at the per-facility prices above, or the Client's invitation prices) take effect on the Client's next monthly billing date, unless both parties agree in writing to an earlier date.
  • Not included: fees do not include third-party costs the Client pays directly (for example: domain registration, rental-software fees, or ad spend).

2. The Client Owns Everything

  • Everything built or managed under this Agreement belongs to the Client: the website code, the domain name, the hosting account, the Google Business Profile, and every other account or listing.
  • They are created in, or remain in, the Client's name.
  • If this Agreement ends for any reason, the Client keeps all of it, and the website keeps running.
  • The Provider's only rights are the revocable access described in Section 4.

3. Satisfaction Guarantee

  • Website Build: if the Client is not satisfied with the new website within 60 days after it goes live, the Provider will revise it until the Client is satisfied — or refund the Website Build fee in full. The Client keeps the website either way.
  • Monthly plans: cancel anytime, no questions asked, effective at the end of the current billing month. Switch between Maintain and Maintain + Improve anytime, in either direction, at no charge (Section 1).
  • What it covers: this guarantee covers the Provider's fees only — not third-party costs already incurred on the Client's behalf (such as domain registration).

4. Access, Cooperation, and Authorization

  • Access: the Provider can only fix what the Provider can reach. The Client agrees to grant the access described at onboarding (Google Business Profile manager access, domain registrar access, website and rental-software access as applicable) within 7 days of signing.
  • Timelines: timelines in Schedule A are measured from the date access is complete; delays caused by missing access extend those timelines and are not a breach by the Provider.
  • Authorization: the Client authorizes the Provider to act on the Client's behalf to create, claim, correct, and manage the Client's business listings, website, DNS records, and related accounts. All such access is revocable by the Client at any time.
  • Protection: the Provider will never move ownership of any Client account to the Provider, and will not share Client credentials except through the agreed secure vault.

5. Search Results — What the Provider Does Not Promise

  • Google and other search engines control their own results, and no honest provider can promise a specific ranking, a position on a map, or a number of calls or rentals.
  • The Provider promises the work described in Schedule A, performed with reasonable skill and care, and will report results honestly — including before-and-after documentation.
  • Any examples of past results shared with the Client are history, not a promise.

6. Client Information and Content

7. Third-Party Platforms

  • The Provider is not responsible for their actions, policy changes, suspensions, downtime, or pricing, and will help the Client respond to such events as part of the services but cannot control them.

Schedule A — Service Descriptions

  • Website Build (one-time). A complete website for the Client's facility, built in the Client's name: pages for the facility, unit sizes and prices, and relevant services (such as boat/RV storage where applicable); accurate business information and photos supplied by the Client; connection to the Client's existing rental/payment system; technical search-engine foundations (titles, descriptions, structured business data, sitemap, search-console registration); and correction of the Client's core business listings (Google Business Profile, plus major directories). Target timeline: live within 2–3 weeks of access being complete (Section 4).
  • Maintain plan (monthly). Keeps everything running and correct: hosting and domain watched; unlimited small content changes (prices, hours, photos, wording) with reasonable turnaround; listings re-checked monthly; and a monthly summary of what was done.
  • Maintain + Improve plan (monthly). Everything in Maintain, plus active improvement each month: at least one meaningful site or listing improvement; regular Google Business Profile posts; a review-request system and guidance; and ranking/visibility tracking reported to the Client.
  • What the Client provides. Accurate business details and prices; photos; access per Section 4; and timely answers to questions that block work.
