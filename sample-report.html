Free Visibility Report: a 4.9-star facility in a Midwest college town

This is the actual report format you'll receive. This facility has one of the best reputations in its city — 114 Google reviews averaging 4.9 stars — and pays ~$90/month for a website from a major storage-software company. Here's what 20 minutes of looking found.

Finding 1 — Visibility

Not on page 1 for "storage units [your city]"

The money search in your market. Page 1 belongs to two national chains, three directory sites — and a local competitor who uses the same website software you do, holding two spots. The software isn't the problem. The setup is.

Finding 2 — Missing pages

Your title says "Boat and RV Storage" — but there's no boat page and no RV page

The website is four pages deep: Home, Rent, Map, Contact. Every service you sell is one sentence on the homepage. Google can't rank pages that don't exist — and your competitors' dedicated pages outrank your sentence every time.

Finding 3 — Wasted reputation

114 five-star reviews. Zero shown on your website.

Your single most persuasive sales asset is invisible to website visitors — the site shows a generic award badge instead. Embedding your real Google reviews is a one-hour change that converts lookers into renters.

Finding 4 — The college miss

30,000 students within 3 miles. Zero student-storage content.

Every May, thousands of students need summer storage. Right now the national chains harvest all of it, because nothing on your site tells Google — or a student — that you solve that problem.

Finding 5 — Invisible to rich results

No structured data, despite publishing prices

You already publish unit prices — rare and smart. But without schema markup, Google can't show them in search results. You're eligible for enhanced listings and not claiming them.

What's working

Credit where due

Your Google Business Profile is claimed and thriving (that 4.9 didn't happen by accident), your citations are mostly consistent, and prices are published. The foundation is good — it's the visibility layer that was never built.

What I'd do about it (Cleanup + content tier)

Fix the metadata and add schema sitewide · build the boat, RV, and student-storage pages · embed your reviews · correct the two directory listings with stale info · set up the monthly listing watchdog. Fixed price, listed deliverables, before/after report with screenshots — and this report stays free either way.
