Welcome aboard — here's what happens next

Thank you for signing up. If you paid online, Stripe emails your receipt to the address you used at checkout. Scott will contact you within 1 business day to book your 30-minute Access Day call.

Get these ready before the call

The faster these come in, the faster your new site goes live. Photos from your phone are fine.

  • Your unit sizes and current prices (a photo of your rate sheet works)
  • Exact door and inside measurements for your common unit sizes
  • What's actually on site: gate, cameras, lighting, paving, 24/7 access, month-to-month
  • Your true gate hours and office/phone hours
  • 10–20 photos: entrance and sign, drive lanes, an open empty unit, the doors
  • Two or three sentences on who owns the place, since when, and why
  • The login emails you use for Google (your business listing) and wherever you bought your web address

On the Access Day call

  • You add Scott as a Manager on your Google Business Profile — you stay the owner
  • You give delegate access to your domain account (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
  • Access to your current website and rental software, if you have them

You click every button yourself, every account stays in your name, and you can remove Scott's access anytime. Passwords are never sent by text or email.

Launch day

Your new site goes live, and your monthly plan (if you chose one) starts that day — you'll get a secure link to set it up, then it renews on the same day each month. Cancel anytime; you keep everything.
